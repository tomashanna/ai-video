⭐⭐ THE #1 SCORING DIMENSION — EXECUTION CONTINUITY (30% weight)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The single biggest factor separating canonical viral Reels (9.5-10) from amateur productions (7-8) is **EXECUTION CONTINUITY** — the viewer experiences a PHYSICALLY CONTINUOUS journey, where each shot's motion, audio, and POV position bridges seamlessly to the next.

### Definition — what EXECUTION CONTINUITY is (NOT narrative continuity)

NARRATIVE CONTINUITY = "Do the events make sense in story logic?" (Gemini is naturally good at this — usually 9+ even in flawed videos.)

EXECUTION CONTINUITY = "Does the viewer PHYSICALLY FEEL one continuous journey?"

Specifically, 4 criteria:

1. **POV/Character traversal motion REAL between shots** — Camera/character is PHYSICALLY MOVING during transitions. The viewer SEES themselves walking, descending, advancing. NEVER teleportation between locations.

2. **Audio bridges across cuts** — Sound layer (drone/ambient/foley) is CONTINUOUS across edit points. Music doesn't drop at cuts. Sound matches the physical journey.

3. **Visual style consistent shot-to-shot** — Color grading, lighting, framing language stays consistent. No jarring style jumps.

4. **Kinetic momentum sustains** — The motion of shot N continues into shot N+1. If POV is walking forward in shot N, shot N+1 shows continued forward motion OR organic stop. NOT abrupt teleport to different setup.

### Critical examples

**❌ EXECUTION CONTINUITY BROKEN (Tomás scored matadero v1 = 7.9 because of this)**:
```
Shot 1: meat hanging close-up (POV stationary observing)
  ↓ HARD CUT (no motion bridge, audio drops)
Shot 2: matadero exterior wide (POV teleported, suddenly different framing)
  ↓ HARD CUT (no traversal)
Shot 3: cutting POV (POV teleported to action — where in space am I?)
  ↓ HARD CUT
Shot 4: monstrous mouth appears (teleport again, no spatial link)
  ↓ HARD CUT
Shot 5: mouth attacks (teleport to consequence)

→ STORY makes sense (you work at matadero → you see disturbing things → you get attacked).
→ EXECUTION is BROKEN — every cut is a TELEPORT, no physical journey through space, no kinetic momentum.
→ Viewer's brain narratively understands but BODY perceives disconnected slideshow.
→ Result: viewer feels "lost" despite story logic working.
```

**✅ EXECUTION CONTINUITY achieved (Tomás scored Ref-A cave = 10)**:
```
Shot 1: I'm in the cave (POV stationary, ambient audio drone)
  ↓ POV WALKS FORWARD (kinetic motion bridges cut, audio sustained)
Shot 2: deeper in cave (visible continuation of motion, same audio layer)
  ↓ Diving suit offered (POV stops at character, motion organic transition)
Shot 3: putting on suit (POV transitions to action, audio continues)
  ↓ I descend (POV motion downward visible, music shifts but layer continuous)
Shot 4: underwater POV (visible continuation of descent, underwater audio bridges)
  ↓ Swimming forward (motion continues, narrative + execution aligned)
Shot 5: encounter (organic stop, audio layer sustained, atmosphere continuous)

→ STORY logic + EXECUTION continuity BOTH present.
→ Viewer physically FEELS the journey.
→ Result: 10/10.
```

### Scoring rule for EXECUTION CONTINUITY (dimension 1, weight 30%)

- **10/10**: Every shot transition has at least 2 of {POV motion bridge / continuous audio / kinetic momentum / consistent style}. Viewer feels ONE continuous physical journey. POV traversal visibly present.

- **8/10**: Most transitions have continuity bridges. 1-2 hard-cut teleportations but mostly bridged.

- **6/10**: Half transitions have bridges, half are teleportations. Viewer feels journey "stop-and-start".

- **4/10**: Most transitions are teleportations between shots. Story chain present BUT physical journey absent. POV is observer-static between cuts. ⚠️ THIS IS THE MATADERO PATTERN.

- **2/10**: Total slideshow — every shot is its own setup, no transitions, no journey.

### Anti-pattern that triggers HARD penalty here

If you identify 3+ cuts in the video where:
- POV/character does NOT physically move between shots
- Audio layer breaks at the cut (silence or unrelated sound)
- The location feels "teleported to" not "walked into"

→ Score this dimension at 4-6 maximum, NOT higher. Story chain coherence DOES NOT compensate.
